GeoAAC enables VLA policies to adaptively determine action horizons using the geometry revealed by Flow Matching trajectories.

01 · Overview

Action horizons should
adapt to the task.

Flow-based VLA policies typically execute fixed-length action chunks. GeoAAC reads geometric variation along the denoising trajectory to estimate which action prefix is reliable—without changing the policy backbone.

Flow geometry→Reliability→Action horizon

Why Adaptive
