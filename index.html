GeoAAC uses prefix-wise geometry from a single Flow Matching generation to select an adaptive action horizon—without additional training.

01 · Overview

Action horizons should
adapt to the task.

Fixed action horizons cannot accommodate changing requirements for continuity, precision, and closed-loop feedback. GeoAAC constructs a horizon-wise geometric profile from prefix-wise denoising trajectories and selects an execution boundary from its cumulative relative growth.

The method operates on a single Flow Matching generation, requires no additional training, and consistently improves over fixed-horizon and existing adaptive baselines across LIBERO, LIBERO-Pro, RoboCasa365, and real-world manipulation.

07 · Real robot

Real-world Deployment

Across three tasks, GeoAAC raises average success from 53.3% with Fixed-50 to 74.4% using the same policy checkpoint.

Hardware

Dual-arm PiperX platform

Two PiperX arms and three Intel RealSense D435i cameras provide one third-person and two wrist-mounted views. The Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct-based policy predicts 50-step chunks and controls the robot at 50 Hz.
